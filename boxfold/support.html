Frequently asked

How do I play?

Draw across 6 connected squares that can fold into a cube. If they fold, the box is cut out. Clear a level by cutting the whole sheet into boxes with nothing left over. Squares with coffee stains can’t be used.

My 6 squares didn’t make a box

The game folds them for you and marks in red the two faces that land on the same side. Try another shape: only 11 of the 6-square shapes fold into a cube.

I’m stuck on a level

Tap Hint. The first tap points out a cut that isn’t part of the answer, or the square to start from; the next tap outlines one box of the answer. Hints are unlimited and free. Every level has exactly one solution.

What are Timed and Endless mode?

A strip of paper keeps feeding upward. Cut as many boxes as you can before squares scroll off unused. Timed mode lasts 120 seconds and is free. Endless mode is part of the full version and adds stationery items, gold paper and combos; the run ends when the waste bin is full.

What does the full version include?

All 60 levels, Endless mode, and any levels added in later updates. It is a single one-time purchase: no subscription and no ads.

I bought the full version but it isn’t unlocked

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, and make sure you are signed in with the Apple account used for the purchase.

Will my progress move to a new device?

Progress and scores are saved on the device only. The purchase itself follows your Apple account and can be restored on any device.
