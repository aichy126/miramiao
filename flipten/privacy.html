Privacy Policy for Flipten

Last updated: 2026-10-10

This Privacy Policy describes how miramiao (“we”, “us”; www.miramiao.com) handles your information when you play Flipten (“the game”).

The short version

We do not collect, store, or receive any of your data. Flipten has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. The game runs entirely on your device.

What stays on your device

Your chapter and puzzle progress, your fastest chapter times, your Endless mode top 10, and your settings (sound volume, haptics, skin and language) are saved inside the game on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Network

The game connects to the internet only to talk to the App Store: to show the price of the full version, to complete a purchase, and to restore purchases. Nothing else is sent or received. Links to our website open in your browser.

Permissions

The game does not ask for any permissions.

Purchases

If you buy the full version, the transaction is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the game saves is stored only on your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. To remove all of it, delete the game; iOS removes its storage with it. A purchase is tied to your Apple account and can be restored after reinstalling.

Tracking

The game does not track you, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not share data with other companies for advertising or analytics.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes are reflected in the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@miramiao.com.
