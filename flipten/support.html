Frequently asked

How do I play?

Digits fall onto the board. Tap the first and last digit of a straight line that adds up to 10, and the line clears. Drag a digit onto its neighbor to swap them: both digits flip as they move, like a mirror. Clear enough digits before the board fills up.

What does each digit flip into?

Digits are drawn with seven segments, and a swap mirrors those segments. A 2 becomes a 5, and a 5 becomes a 2. A 0 and an 8 stay the same. A 1 stays a 1 when you swap it up or down, but a sideways swap moves its line to the left, so it is no longer a 1. A 3 stays a 3 up or down. A 6 turns into a 9 (and back) only after one sideways and one up-or-down swap. Shapes that are not digits are shown dimmed and can’t be added until you flip them back.

What happens while a line is fading?

For a moment the game slows down. Tap a glowing digit next to the fading line to make another 10 and keep the chain going.

What is the digit on a solid tile?

It can’t be moved. Make 10 with it, and every digit on the board with the same value clears too.

I’m stuck on a puzzle

Tap Hint to see the next move from where you are now. A puzzle solved with a hint counts as solved but not “at par”. Every puzzle has more than one way to reach the par.

What does the full version include?

All 9 chapters, all 60 puzzles, Endless mode, the Paper skin, and any chapters and puzzles added in later updates. It is a single one-time purchase: no subscription and no ads.

I bought the full version but it isn’t unlocked

In the game, open Settings → Full version and tap Restore purchases, and make sure you are signed in with the Apple account used for the purchase.

Will my progress move to a new device?

Progress and scores are saved on the device only. The purchase itself follows your Apple account and can be restored on any device.
