Privacy Policy for DSH Workspace

Last updated: 2026-10-01

This Privacy Policy describes how miramiao (“we”, “us”; www.miramiao.com) handles your information when you use DSH Workspace (the “App”).

The short version

We do not collect your conversation data through the App. The App has no accounts of ours, advertising, tracking or usage analytics, and we do not operate a server for your conversations. It connects to the DeepSeek Harness server you choose. Speech recognition may use Apple's service, as described below.

The server you connect to

You enter the address of a DeepSeek Harness gateway that you run or someone runs for you. The App communicates directly with that server. Whoever operates it handles and stores sessions, messages, replies, tool output and attachments. We do not receive a copy.

Sign-in and credentials

The App asks for the password you set for the DeepSeek Harness LAN gateway and sends it directly to the server address you entered to sign in. It does not save the password in local settings. After sign-in, the session cookie issued by that server is kept in the device's web data store and used to communicate with that server. The server address and display preferences are stored locally and are not sent to us. On a local network using HTTP, other devices on that network may be able to see the password and cookie in transit. Use HTTPS when connecting across networks.

What the App sends, and when

  • Prompts and attachments — when you send a message, its text, and any photos or files you attached, go to the server you configured. They are chosen through the system photo/file picker; the App does not read your photo library on its own.
  • Session actions — the model you choose, folders you select for new sessions, answers to questions, approval or rejection of tool actions, and other session controls you initiate are sent to that server. When browsing folders, the App reads directory listings from that server.

The App has no automatic crash reporting or usage analytics of ours. Local diagnostics stay on the device unless you choose to send a feedback email with the report attached.

Voice input

Tap the voice button to start recording, then tap again to stop and insert the transcript into the message box. Speech is transcribed with Apple's Speech framework: on the device when supported, or possibly through Apple's speech recognition service otherwise. You can edit the transcript before sending it; the text reaches your DeepSeek Harness server only when you press send. Apple's handling of speech recognition is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions the App may ask for

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — only for the voice input described above.
  • Photos and Files — only to pick what you attach to a message, through the system picker. The App does not request access to your whole library, and does not use the camera.
  • Local network — only if the DSH server you enter is on your own network.

What is kept on your device

Server addresses, list and grouping preferences, recently used folders, login cookies, local diagnostics, and attachments you picked but have not sent yet are kept on the device. Attachments are temporary files, removed when sent or removed from the message box. Deleting the App removes its data from the device.

Tracking

The App does not track you, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not share data with other companies for advertising or analytics.

Children

The App is a tool for operating a software service and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; material changes are reflected in the “Last updated” date above.
