Privacy Policy for DSH Workspace

Last updated: 2026-09-29

This Privacy Policy describes how miramiao (“we”, “us”; www.miramiao.com) handles your information when you use DSH Workspace (the “App”).

The short version

We do not collect, store or receive any of your data. The App has no accounts of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers of our own. The App is a client for a DeepSeek Harness (DSH) server that you point it at — your own instance, or one someone else runs for you. Everything you do in the App travels between your device and that server, and nowhere else.

The server you connect to

On first run you enter the address of a DSH web workspace, and you sign in through that workspace's own web page. The App then talks directly to that server. What the server stores — sessions, prompts, replies, tool output, uploaded attachments — is controlled by whoever operates that server, not by us. If you run it yourself, that is you. We never receive a copy and have no access to it.

Sign-in and credentials

The App does not ask for a new password and does not create an account. It uses the login session issued by the DSH web workspace you signed in to, kept on your device in the app's web data store (cookies), and presents it back to that same server so the native screens can read your sessions. The server address and your screen preferences are kept in the App's local settings on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

What the App sends, and when

  • Prompts and attachments — when you send a message, its text, and any photos or files you attached, go to the server you configured. They are chosen through the system photo/file picker; the App does not read your photo library on its own.
  • Answers to questions the agent asks — when a session asks you to choose an option, your answer is sent back to that server.

Nothing else leaves your device. The App has no third-party SDKs, no crash reporting of ours and no usage statistics.

Voice input

The microphone is used only while you are holding the voice button. Speech is transcribed with Apple's Speech framework: on the device whenever the system supports on-device recognition, and otherwise through Apple's speech recognition service — the App tells you on screen when that is the case. The transcript is put into the message box for you to edit; nothing is sent to your server until you press send. Apple's handling of speech recognition is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Permissions the App may ask for

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — only for the voice input described above.
  • Photos and Files — only to pick what you attach to a message, through the system picker. The App does not request access to your whole library, and does not use the camera.
  • Local network — only if the DSH server you enter is on your own network.

What is kept on your device

The server address, your list and grouping preferences, the folders you used recently, the web login cookies described above, and any attachments you picked but have not sent yet (those are temporary files, removed when you send them or remove them from the message box). Deleting the App deletes all of it.

Tracking

The App does not track you, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not share data with other companies for advertising or analytics.

Children

The App is a tool for operating a software service and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; material changes are reflected in the “Last updated” date above.
