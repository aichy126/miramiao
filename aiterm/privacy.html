Privacy Policy for aiterm

Last updated: 2026-10-12

This Privacy Policy describes how miramiao (“we”, “us”; www.miramiao.com) handles your information when you use aiterm (the “App”).

The short version

We do not collect any data through the App. The App has no accounts of ours, advertising, tracking or usage analytics, and we do not operate a server for it. It connects only to the aiterm server you choose.

The server you connect to

You enter the address of an aiterm server that you run or someone runs for you. The App communicates directly with that server. Terminal input and output, files, and conversation history are handled and stored by whoever operates it. We do not receive a copy.

Sign-in and credentials

The App signs in with a challenge-response exchange, so your password is not sent to the server and is not saved on the device. The sign-in tokens issued by the server are stored in the device Keychain. If you import a client certificate (.p12), the certificate and its password are also stored in the Keychain, and are presented only to servers that ask for one. When you trust a server's self-signed certificate, its fingerprint is stored so the App can detect if that certificate changes.

What the App sends, and when

  • Terminal input — what you type or paste in a terminal goes to your server.
  • Files — photos and files you pick through the system picker, images you paste into a terminal, and edits you save in the file editor are uploaded to your server. The App does not read your photo library on its own.
  • Share extension — text, links, images and files you share to aiterm from other apps are sent to your server.

Permissions the App may ask for

  • Camera — only to scan the pairing QR code shown by your aiterm server. Images are not saved or sent.
  • Local network — only to connect to an aiterm server on your own network.

What is kept on your device

Recently used server addresses, appearance preferences, recently used folders, and the Keychain items described above. Deleting the App removes its data from the device; Keychain items may remain until you reset or erase the device.

Tracking

The App does not track you, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not share data with other companies for advertising or analytics.

Children

The App is a tool for operating your own server and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; material changes are reflected in the “Last updated” date above.
