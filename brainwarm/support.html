Frequently asked

What is Brainwarm?

Twenty short practices across five areas: focus, memory, self-control, math, and space. Each takes a minute or two. Brainwarm is designed for people with ADHD, but anyone can use it. It is a practice tool, not a medical test.

What is today's focus?

A check of about two minutes made of three fixed practices. Your score is compared only with your own recent checks, so you can see whether today is a sharper day than usual. There are no ages, ranks, or comparisons with other people.

How does the difficulty work?

Every practice has 6 levels. Do well and it goes up a level next time; find it hard and it comes back down. You don't need to choose anything.

What does the full version include?

The free version includes today's focus every day and one practice in each of the five areas. The full version unlocks all 20 practices, and your daily set and five-area check then draw from all of them. It is a one-time purchase: not a subscription, no ads.

I bought the full version but it's still locked

Open Settings → Purchase in the app, tap Restore purchases, and make sure you are signed in with the Apple account you used to buy it.

I don't hear any sound

Sound follows your device's silent switch and plays alongside your music. Check the switch and the volume, and make sure Sound effects is on in Settings.

Will my records move to a new phone?

Your records are stored only on your device, with no account or sync of ours. They come along if you set up the new device from a backup of the old one. Your purchase follows your Apple account and can be restored on any device.
